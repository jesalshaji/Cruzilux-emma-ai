# Day Zero

**Date**

2026-08-06

## What We Achieved

- Established the long-term vision of the CruziLux AI Platform.
- Decided that Emma is the first digital employee, not just a chatbot.
- Defined the philosophy of building digital employees with unique personalities.
- Designed the future documentation structure.
- Planned the separation between the public Emma Demo and the future private CruziLux AI Platform.
- Defined the company isolation principle.
- Defined the character system.

- Emma can respond using voice.
- Frontend and backend communicate successfully using WebSockets.
- Documentation sprint officially started.
